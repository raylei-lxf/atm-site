# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 9, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how we ("we", "us") handle information in connection with the **Agent Task Manager** VS Code extension (the "Extension") and licenses purchased for it.

## 1. Information we collect

- **Purchase information.** Payments are processed by our partner **Creem** (creem.io), which acts as the Merchant of Record. We receive order details such as your **email address, order number, Device Code, and purchase status**. We never receive or store your full card details.
- **Device Code.** To issue your license, you provide a **Device Code** — a one-way hash derived from your machine's hardware identifier. You enter it at checkout (the required "Device code" field on the Creem order form). It cannot be used to identify you personally, and it is used only to bind your license to your device.
- **Support communications.** If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

## 2. How we use this information

- To issue, deliver, and verify your license (including free re-issuance for the same device);
- To provide customer support and process refunds;
- To comply with legal obligations.

## 3. What the Extension does

- License verification happens **offline, on your own computer**. The Extension does not send usage data, telemetry, or analytics to us.
- To display the correct local price, the Extension may determine your approximate region (for example, from your IP address) using a third-party lookup service. Only your natural outbound IP address is exposed; the result is cached locally on your device for up to 24 hours and is not stored or shared by us.

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

- **Creem** — for payment processing, order management, and license issuance (including your Device Code);
- **Email providers** — for support communications;
- authorities, when required by law.

## 5. Retention

License records (order number, Device Code, license ID, issue date) are kept as long as needed for license recovery and legal compliance.

## 6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing **raylei483@gmail.com**. Deleting license records may limit our ability to re-issue your license for the same device.
